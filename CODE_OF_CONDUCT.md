# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, discuss evidence rather than people, accept well-supported corrections, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or publication of private information.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and private correspondence about the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `info@howmuchmusic.com`. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe and evidence-led project.
